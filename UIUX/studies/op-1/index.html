A small instrument. A complete control language.

Enochian Synth Keyboard, recast as a pocket studio. Press and hold a note, shape it with four encoders, then capture a phrase on the note-event tape. A study of physical interaction — not a hardware emulation.

01 / PLAYING GUIDE

Sound is off on arrival. Enable the small master button to create a Web Audio context. Notes remain visible when muted. This study records note events in memory, not audio; refreshing clears your tape.

Keyboard & touch

First octave: A S D F G H J white keys; W E T Y U accidentals. Second: K L ; ' ] \\ / white; O P [ = Backspace accidentals. Focus any note then hold Space or Enter. Glide while holding; independent touch contacts and computer chords coexist. Z / X shift the octave. Leaving the window releases all notes.

Encoders & tape

Drag upwards to increase. Arrow keys change one step; Page Up / Down change ten; Home / End set the limits. Double-click returns that encoder to the Canticle default. Presets 1–8 are real parameter sets. Record arms a new take; Play starts capture. Stop finishes it, then Play replays it. Loop repeats a take. Clear has one-level undo. Home stops and rewinds.

No sequencer, mixer, audio export, or hardware firmware is implied. Playback uses your current sound settings and the original recorded MIDI pitches.
