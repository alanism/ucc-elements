Operation & construction notes

Start with Play demo, or the Twin’s top-rail play switch. Volume: drag upward, use arrow keys, Page Up / Down, Home / End, or double-click to reset to 35%. Input cycles Chord, Pulse, and Noise. Tape toggles looping. Every sound is generated locally; these are audio-control studies, not microphone recorders.

Geometry is drawn in CSS and SVG. Rotary and damped spring behavior are adapted from OP and the Volume studies; transport state from Volume 2; the bounded Web Audio bus from Volume 3’s Canticle. Sound never resumes automatically after leaving the page.
