Elements

Tactile controls for the interfaces arriving now: personalized AI, and the smart devices you drive far from a laptop. Flat design had a good decade. Skeuomorphism is returning, in the softer form the industry now calls neumorphism — and these are the working studies.

Purpose
One job per control, and the state it is in stays visible.
Use
Panels, mini-apps, and device surfaces you touch away from the laptop.
How
Turn, flip, press, drag. Pointer or keyboard both work; each control takes an Ivory or an Obsidian finish; open the Code Veil to copy the pattern.
